# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with React islands, Tailwind CSS, and Motion. Static output is the current target. The language model is extensible; Catalan is the default and Spanish is the first additional locale.

## Users

Inferred from the brief: people discovering Poblaliment for the first time, and potential suppliers or collaborators looking for context and a way to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Inferred from the brief: present Poblaliment, explain who it is, introduce its suppliers, and make contact easy through a clear four-page public website.

## Positioning

Open decision: the specific positioning, proof points, and differentiator of Poblaliment still need to be supplied.

## Operating Context

The first release is a static multilingual website. Catalan is the primary reading experience; Spanish is available from the header language switcher.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four initial routes: Inici, Nosaltres, Les nostres marques, and Contacte.
- The section currently called “Les nostres marques” should be easy to rename to “Proveïdors” in future copy and navigation updates.
- The locale architecture must be extensible beyond Catalan and Spanish.
- Motion is reserved for purposeful entrance and interaction feedback, with reduced-motion support.
- Real business claims, contact details, logo, and supplier data are open decisions and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

The working name is Poblaliment. No visual identity, typography, or colour system has been confirmed yet.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a collection of food and product photographs under `images/`. They are available as source material, but their final subjects, licensing, and editorial use have not been confirmed.

## Product Principles

- Make the local product relationship understandable quickly.
- Put Catalan first without making Spanish feel secondary or unfinished.
- Keep content and locale data separate from route components.
- Use a small, dependable visual system that can grow with real brand material.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The scaffold should provide semantic landmarks, keyboard-visible focus, usable contrast, responsive layouts, and a reduced-motion path.
